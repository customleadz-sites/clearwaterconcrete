reinforce, pour.

08 — Good Questions

Before you call.

What kinds of concrete work do you do?

Flatwork: shop floors and pads, building slabs, driveways, approaches, patios, porches and sidewalks.

Do you repair cracked concrete?

We don't patch cracks or do small spot repairs. If a driveway is worn out, we tear it out and pour a new one.

What finishes can I choose from?

Slick, fuzzy, light broom or heavy broom. We'll recommend one based on how the concrete will be used and how steep it is.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. We look at the site, measure, talk through thickness, reinforcement and finish, and give you a price before any work starts.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Clear Water Concrete is licensed and insured.

How is the price figured?

Size, thickness, how much grading the site needs, reinforcement, and the finish you want. You get one number up front.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and what's already on the schedule. Tell us when you need it done and we'll give you a straight answer with the estimate.

What areas do you cover?

Augusta, Evans, Martinez, Grovetown, Hephzibah and Harlem in Georgia, plus North Augusta, South Carolina.
